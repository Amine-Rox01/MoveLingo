## English educator

- Is the language objective appropriate for this group and material?
- Does the task require a real information gap, choice or problem with a checkable outcome?
- Are model text, role cards, expected answers and assessment accurate and complete?
- Do listening/reading/writing/speaking procedures match the selected skill?
- Are student-facing instructions understandable? What misconceptions are likely?
- Can the teacher run the task without inventing missing content?

## Qualified PE educator

- Is there an observable physical-learning criterion beyond being active?
- Are movement demands, group rotations and active lanes appropriate to the usable space?
- Are equipment, stop signal, supervision and comfortable progression specified?
- Does the agreed seated/hand/token option preserve meaningful participation and decision-making?
- Are physical and language observations separated?
