All material is included in this file. Independent English/PE review and classroom evaluation are pending. Private role cards and answer keys are for teacher-controlled distribution.

English: Identify three movement instructions in a short spoken message, request repetition and place the actions in the correct order.
Physical: Perform or trace three comfortable actions in the agreed order, with a controlled stop between actions and an agreed personal-space boundary.
Layout: Groups of three: coach, performer and observer. Give each group a checked personal-space area and a desk version. No running, body contact or jumping. Rotate roles after one short sequence. For one learner the teacher is coach and observer.

All stages

1. Listen, respond and stop

5 minutes · TPR warm-up · Pairs

Say this

Watch the comfortable actions. Listen: stop, reach, stop, turn, stop. Copy the action or use your hand or token version. Keep your own space.

Procedure

  1. 0–1 min · Model the three comfortable actions without asking learners to speak: reach, turn towards a marker, stop. Offer a hand-trace or token version.
  2. 1–3 min · Say “stop, reach, stop, turn, stop”. Learners listen and respond in the checked personal-space area.
  3. 3–5 min · Give the actions in a new order. Check controlled stops; repeat the cue when meaning is unclear.

Teacher role

  • Model the supplied language or action, then check understanding with one learner.
  • Observe the English and physical criteria separately; record support and use the agreed access version.

Learner role

  • Attend to the model and use the supplied language or picture cards.
  • Take a meaningful turn, check the outcome and help partners clarify uncertainty.

2. Discover the model

8 minutes · Model & notice · Pairs

Say this

Listen first without the script. What is the situation? Now answer: What are the three actions, and which action does the performer ask to hear again? Compare with a partner before checking the answer.

Procedure

  1. 0–3 min · Teacher reads the model twice before showing its script; first listen for the situation, then listen for the action order.
  2. 3–6 min · Answer: What are the three actions, and which action does the performer ask to hear again?
  3. 6–8 min · Compare answers with the teacher key: Reach up; turn towards the triangle; stop. The performer asks to hear the turn instruction again.

3. Listen and order the action cards

8 minutes · Guided practice · Pairs

Say this

Listen to the coach. Put REACH, TURN and STOP in the order you hear. Ask for a repeat if needed. Check the script, then try the comfortable sequence.

Procedure

  1. 0–3 min · Read the original coach model twice. Learners listen first without seeing the script, then place REACH, TURN and STOP picture cards in order.
  2. 3–6 min · Read again. Partners compare their order and use “Please repeat the second action” to resolve uncertainty.
  3. 6–8 min · Reveal the script and check REACH → TURN → STOP. Rehearse once using comfortable actions or desk tokens.

4. Listen, check and coach

17 minutes · Communicative movement task · Groups

Say this

Coach, secretly choose one action order. Read it slowly without showing your card. Performer, listen first and ask for an action again. Put the cards in order, then do the comfortable actions or move your token. Observer, check the order and the stops. Show the script and change roles.

Procedure

  1. 0–3 min · Give the coach the private script, the performer three mixed picture cards and the observer the separate key. Keep the script hidden until checking.
  2. 3–13 min · The coach reads; the performer requests repetition, orders the cards and then performs or traces the sequence. The observer checks order and controlled stops.
  3. 13–17 min · Reveal the script, repair one mismatch and rotate roles. In the next round the coach says TURN → REACH → STOP, and the observer uses that announced order.

Teacher role

  • Distribute the private roles and demonstrate the information-gap rule without revealing the answer.
  • Observe the English and physical criteria separately; record support and use the agreed access version.

Learner role

  • Exchange missing information before deciding and moving.
  • Take a meaningful turn, check the outcome and help partners clarify uncertainty.

5. Explain, check and improve

7 minutes · Report & repair · Pairs

Say this

Show your result. Explain one choice or one unclear part. Ask another group one useful question. Improve the result and show what you changed.

Procedure

  1. 0–2 min · Groups report which instruction needed repetition and show their final card order.
  2. 2–5 min · Read the changed round TURN → REACH → STOP. Invite one group to explain how they checked the difference.
  3. 5–7 min · Rehearse the unclear action cue once and name a useful listening strategy: listen first, ask, then act.

6. Independent evidence check

5 minutes · Exit check · Individual

Say this

Listen to the new short message. Put your three cards in order, then show or trace the sequence. Ask for repetition if you need it. Your teacher checks the listening order and the movement separately.

Procedure

  1. 0–1 min · Without showing a script, read: “First, turn towards the marker. Then, reach up comfortably. Finally, stop.”
  2. 1–3 min · Each learner independently orders the three cards and demonstrates or traces the order. A repetition request is allowed and recorded as support.
  3. 3–5 min · Record the correct listening order separately from controlled movement. Choose one focused rehearsal for learners who need it.

Teacher role
