# Five-minute demonstration and questions

0:00–0:40 — State the teacher problem and show the separate English and physical criteria.
0:40–1:40 — Show private cards and an exact worked-example schematic; hide the private example in teaching mode.
1:40–2:40 — Change a real objective/space/support constraint and generate a live adaptation. If unavailable, explicitly switch to the authored offline pack.
2:40–3:40 — Record fictional demonstration observations clearly labelled as demo inputs, or actual anonymized approved pilot data. Show the denominators, approve a support suggestion and open the separate adapted lesson.
3:40–5:00 — Discuss actual evidence if available and its limitations. Show the self-contained teacher pack and explain provider/data boundaries.

Prepare answers: Why movement? What physical learning? Why this task structure? What does AI change? How are private cards protected? What happens offline? What data are sent? What classroom evidence exists? What can the current evidence not establish?

Never present prepared data as real results or an authored pack as a live AI response.
