# MoveLingo: methods and limitations

MoveLingo is an AI-assisted teacher workspace for planning, demonstrating, observing and adapting English learning through purposeful movement. Its original teaching packs combine Total Physical Response routines with task-supported communicative language practice; not every stage is a pure task-based lesson cycle.

## What AI does

Gemini receives teacher-selected objectives, context, samples, space, equipment, layout/group constraints and approved observation evidence. It returns a structured draft. Runtime checks cover schema, timing, selected skill, quotation matching and required movement fields. These checks do not establish factual, pedagogical or physical-safety correctness. An educator reviews before teaching.

Teacher-recorded feedback ratings describe support needed in a particular task. Charts calculate counts from those observations; they are not AI measurements, diagnoses, rankings or validated proficiency grades. Missing observations are excluded. Local support suggestions identify their rating evidence and require teacher approval before adapting a separate copy of the lesson.

## Provenance and scope

The 20 ready adaptations and three showcase packs use original model texts and role cards. Spotlight unit names are thematic references; exact textbook pages and full national alignment remain unverified until a teacher supplies and checks them. Realistic generated photos depict fictional people. They are not classroom-trial evidence. Schematics explain task mechanics and need teacher modelling.

## Current boundaries

No learning gains, independent expert approval or classroom trials are established by this software release. Institutional use and minors' data require appropriate local arrangements. Gemini free-tier availability and provider policies may change. Anonymous observations and teaching material are sent only on requested AI generation; keep sensitive information out. Optional local drafts and saved sessions are device-specific and need exported backups. The full site is not an offline AI system; downloaded teacher packs are self-contained and clearly authored, with no live generation.

The local/private deployment uses identity checks and per-worker usage throttles. A public multi-user launch needs a configured shared durable quota, provider budget controls, access review and institutional data policy. This build should not be represented as a public-production certification.
