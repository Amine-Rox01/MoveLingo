Let’s Celebrate! — Sports day: guide the visitor

AUTHORED OFFLINE TEACHER PACK · NO LIVE AI
All material is included in this file. Independent English/PE review and classroom evaluation are pending. Private role cards and answer keys are for teacher-controlled distribution.

English: Give three clear direction instructions, ask for clarification and confirm the correct sports-day destination.
Physical: Follow a marked two-turn route at walking pace or with agreed desk tokens; control each direction change, stop at the signal and respect the checked lane.
Layout: Use a checked 3 × 2 metre lane with paper START, STAR, TRIANGLE, HALL and DESK markers. One group uses each available lane; other groups rehearse with desk maps. Groups of four rotate guide, visitor, language observer and movement observer. Teacher confirms actual usable space before starting.

All stages

1. Hear a direction, control a turn

5 minutes · TPR warm-up · Pairs

Say this

Listen and watch. Move or point only when you hear the direction. Stop when I say stop. Now take turns giving two directions.

Procedure

  1. 0–1 min · Model STOP: hold a hand still; FORWARD: trace a short line; LEFT/RIGHT: turn a finger or body towards the named side. Learners first watch, then respond to the spoken cue.
  2. 1–3 min · Give six cues in a new order: forward, stop, left, stop, right, stop. Everyone responds in their own checked space or with a desk token.
  3. 3–5 min · Pairs take turns giving two cues. Observe controlled stops and meaning, not speed.

Teacher role

  • Model the supplied language or action, then check understanding with one learner.
  • Observe the English and physical criteria separately; record support and use the agreed access version.

Learner role

  • Attend to the model and use the supplied language or picture cards.
  • Take a meaningful turn, check the outcome and help partners clarify uncertainty.

2. Discover the model

8 minutes · Model & notice · Pairs

Say this

Read the model. What is the situation? Now answer: Where does the visitor turn left, and where does the route finish? Compare with a partner before checking the answer.

Procedure

  1. 0–3 min · Read the model once for the situation, then identify useful language.
  2. 3–6 min · Answer: Where does the visitor turn left, and where does the route finish?
  3. 6–8 min · Compare answers with the teacher key: Turn left at the star; finish at the hall.

3. Clarify before moving

8 minutes · Guided practice · Pairs

Say this

Use the practice map. Guide, give the direction. Visitor, ask “Do you mean left?” before moving. Check the turn, then change roles.

Procedure

  1. 0–3 min · Give both partners the practice route START → STAR → TRIANGLE. Rehearse “Go forward to the star. Turn left towards the triangle.”
  2. 3–6 min · The listener asks “Do you mean left?” before moving or tracing the turn. The guide confirms, then the listener follows.
  3. 6–8 min · Swap guide and listener. Each observer checks one clear English cue and one controlled direction change.

4. Sports day: guide the visitor

17 minutes · Communicative movement task · Groups

Say this

Guide, keep your card private. Silently choose HALL or DESK. Give one direction at a time. Visitor, ask a question before a turn. Move slowly only when you understand. Observers, check the English and the controlled turns separately. Show the card after the check. Change roles.

Procedure

  1. 0–3 min · Give the guide private card A, the visitor card B and observers their separate criteria. The visitor must not see the guide’s secret route. Place markers to match the coordinates on card B.
  2. 3–13 min · The guide gives one instruction at a time. The visitor asks a clarification question before the first turn, then follows the route. Observers record English and physical evidence separately.
  3. 13–17 min · Reveal the route card and compare the reached destination. Rotate all four roles and repeat; use a desk-token version while a lane is occupied.

Teacher role

  • Distribute the private roles and demonstrate the information-gap rule without revealing the answer.
  • Observe the English and physical criteria separately; record support and use the agreed access version.

Learner role

  • Exchange missing information before deciding and moving.
  • Take a meaningful turn, check the outcome and help partners clarify uncertainty.

Physical task and safety

Materials and private cards

Private guide card A

Do not show this card until the check. Your visitor starts at START facing STAR. Privately choose the finish: HALL or DESK. HALL route: START → STAR → TRIANGLE → HALL. DESK route: START → STAR → TRIANGLE → HALL → DESK, with one extra right turn. Do not announce your choice first. Say: Go forward to the star. Turn left towards the triangle. Turn right and stop at the hall. Answer clarification questions. Do not move the visitor yourself.

Visitor card B and map

Your goal: find the sports-day destination secretly assigned by the guide. It may be HALL or DESK. Ask one clarification question before a turn. Move only after you understand. Map viewed from above:
          TRIANGLE ─── HALL
              │
START ───── STAR
Arrange TRIANGLE directly ahead of STAR after its left turn. Arrange HALL to the right of TRIANGLE and DESK directly below HALL. Start facing STAR. Your guide has the route; do not look at it.

Observer cards and check key

Language observer: record clear direction / clarification / destination confirmation (independent, supported or not observed). Movement observer: note controlled turn, response to stop signal, and lane awareness using the agreed adaptation. Correct routes: START → STAR → TRIANGLE → HALL, or continue with a right turn to DESK. Check the guide’s private choice and actual order, not speed. Swap roles after each turn.

5. Explain, check and improve

7 minutes · Report & repair · Pairs

Say this

Show your result. Explain one choice or one unclear part. Ask another group one useful question. Improve the result and show what you changed.

Procedure

  1. 0–2 min · Each group reports its destination and one instruction that needed clarification. Show the route without displaying learner ratings.
  2. 2–5 min · Another group checks the route against the teacher key and asks “Where did you turn?”
  3. 5–7 min · Repeat the unclear instruction and the controlled turn once. Explain what made the direction clearer.

6. Independent evidence check

5 minutes · Exit check · Individual

Say this

Work on your own. Give or write three directions for START → STAR → TRIANGLE → HALL. Include a clarification question or answer. Show the two controlled turns and stop, or use your agreed token version.

Procedure

  1. 0–1 min · Individually give or write three directions for START → STAR → TRIANGLE → HALL. Include a clarification question or a reply to one.
  2. 1–3 min · Show the two controlled turns and final stop, or the agreed token equivalent. The observer records the physical criterion separately from English accuracy.
  3. 3–5 min · Teacher checks the English sequence and physical criterion, then records one support need for the next session.

Teacher role
